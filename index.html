    Your family’s mission

    Your superpower?
    Showing up for others.

    This is an authentic, family-led experience, not a typical school assignment. Children practise empathy, initiative, responsibility and reflection—and discover how their actions contribute to the wider community.

    First-time families can discover a cause together. Returning families can continue, deepen, or explore a new cause.

    Family-led volunteering with a meaningful purpose.
    Three superpowers you’ll grow
    🌍
    Cosmic Education

    We are all connected. Our actions affect the world around us.

    🛠️
    Practical Life

    Real experiences build skills, confidence and independence.

    💛
    Grace and Courtesy

    Respect, kindness and gratitude guide how we work together.

    Choose your adventure

    Choose your trail.

    Is this your first volunteering journey, or are you ready to build on last year?

    Path A · Beginning your volunteering journey

    For families participating for the first time.

    • Explore and research 3–5 possible organisations or initiatives in Valencia.
    • Make contact and agree on a realistic family commitment and schedule.
    • Take action and document the experience.
    • Sustain and reflect.

    Path B · Deepening your impact

    For families who completed the project last year. Choose the direction that fits your family:

    • Continue with the same organisation.
    • Explore a new organisation or cause.
    • Develop a small sustained initiative in response to a genuine community need.

    🔎 Find something you can improve

    1. Identify a need within the organisation and come up with 2–3 possible actions.
    2. Compare each option’s pros, cons, feasibility and risks. Confirm with the organisation that it would be useful.
    3. Choose one realistic project the child can lead with family support.

    Finding opportunities in Valencia

    Families may begin their search through:

    • Valencia City Council’s volunteer platform.
    • Local community centres and other community networks.
    • Websites of established charities and organisations, including local branches of the Red Cross and Cáritas.
    • Organisations connected to environmental protection, animal welfare, education and social support.

    💡 What could my contribution look like?

    Depending on the project, the child might:

    • Send a researched letter or proposal.
    • Share an awareness resource with an appropriate audience.
    • Create a useful resource for the organisation.
    • Organise a small family or community action.
    • Carry out an approved environmental or solidarity initiative.
    • Explore a petition or another form of civic participation, where appropriate.
